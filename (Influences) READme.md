# My inspiration

## Sabrina Carpenter
Sabrina Carpenter is an American singer-songwriter and actress, born on May 11, 1999, in Pennsylvania, who gained prominence starring as Maya Hart in the Disney Channel series Girl Meets World. Her music career includes chart-topping songs like "Feather" and "Espresso" and the album Short n' Sweet (and recently, 'TEARS' in her new 'Man's Best Friend' Album) . Sabrina is recognized for her style and inspiring message for her fans.

She has multiple songs with the 1930s and 1940s, With her recent song 'TEARS' capturing the American Disco era and including activism messages which was extrememly popular around the 1970s for gay rights.
