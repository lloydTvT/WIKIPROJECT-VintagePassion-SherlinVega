#### Sombr
Sombr is the indie pop project of New York City-born musician Shane Michael Boose, who gained significant attention in 2022 with his viral TikTok single **"Caroline"**. After developing music in a home studio and dropping out of LaGuardia High School to pursue his career, Boose released the 2023 EP In Another Life and achieved a major breakthrough in 2025 with his viral singles **"Back to Friends"** and **"Undressed,"** which charted globally. His debut studio album, I Barely Know Her, is set for release in August 2025.

Sombr won his first Moonman during the VMAs, Wearing mystic-inspired Valentino tuxedo featuring an embroidered lapel, a tasseled sash, a striped shirt, and studded pointed boots.
