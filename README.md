## Vintage fashion
Vintage fashion has its ways of coming back in lots of shape or form, Vintage being historical, old and subcultural. Ranging from the 1940s possibly older to modern day, the popularity of finding your style in another year that isn’t your own. People like to believe that the year 2025 was a year where Vintage fashion is coming back to life more than ever with people being open with their love of dressing like they walked out of a 1950s magazine. **Vogue** is one of those companies that bring out the possibility and beautiful flaws of these fashion portals. 

### Vogue
**Vogue** was founded December 17 of 1892, by Aurthor Baldwin Turnure. Building its way up to what it is now in today’s date, having diverse styling, models and weekly magazine issues. It is said that Vogue has the “Vintage” old timey style people crave in the fashion industry. With celebrity collaborations, Vogue models like *Cindy Crawford, Kate Moss, Gigi Hadid and Shalom Harlow* (Who recently made her way back into the light of social media with her edit still surfing around in tiktok). Vogue’s goal is to have artistic expression, Defining Culture and Global authority. 

### Thrifting
The idea of **Thrifting** founded in the 19th and 20th century became popular, going to shops with used clothing and items giving the accessories and clothing you wear the fashionably historical vibe you need to walk down the streets of Manhattan with. Original it was supposed to reduce fabric waste, but eventually became a good to place to find your fashionalble garments for your evening dinning.
