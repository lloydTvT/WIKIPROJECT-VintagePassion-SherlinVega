## Fashionista
<img width="1200" height="675" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/66b0664a-738e-4f86-a57c-6bcfe651cc45" />

A fashionista is a person who is a devoted follower or creator of fashion trends, with a deep interest in clothing, styles, and accessories. They are often stylish, wear high-fashion or unique clothing, and use fashion to express their identity. The term can refer to designers, buyers, bloggers, or anyone who avidly keeps up with fashion and uses it as a lifestyle. 

A "fashionista" is a person, typically a woman, who is keenly interested in fashion and style. Examples of fashionistas can be found in celebrity culture, such as Jennifer Lopez, Blake Lively, and Sarah Jessica Parker, as well as among fashion bloggers and social media influencers like Chiara Ferragni, Aimee Song, and Camila Coelho. The term can also refer to male enthusiasts, called a "fashionisto," and is the name of a popular line of diverse fashion dolls by Barbie

## Designer's 
A designer is a person who plans, creates, and develops the forms, structures, and patterns of new things, applying creativity and skills to make them both functional and visually appealing. They work across many fields, designing anything from fashion and buildings to websites and cars, solving problems by creating the most effective path from idea to finished product.
